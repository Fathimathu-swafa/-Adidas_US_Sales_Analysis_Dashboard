# Adidas_US_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard (Power BI Dashboard)

## objective
A comprehensive view of Adidas US sales across products, retailers, purchase methods, and regions, enabling the identification of top-performing products, sales trends, and underperforming areas. The dashboard aims to support data-driven decisions by visualizing revenue, profit contributions, and operating margins for business growth.
