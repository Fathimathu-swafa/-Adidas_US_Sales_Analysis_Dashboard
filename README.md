# Adidas_US_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard (Power BI Dashboard)

## objective
A comprehensive view of Adidas US sales across products, retailers, purchase methods, and regions, enabling the identification of top-performing products, sales trends, and underperforming areas. The dashboard aims to support data-driven decisions by visualizing revenue, profit contributions, and operating margins for business growth.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/Fathimathu-swafa/-Adidas_US_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard/blob/main/Adidas%20US%20Sales%20Datasets.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## KPIs
-	Which products generate the highest sales revenue?
-	What are the top-performing retailers by sales revenue?
-	How do different purchase methods contribute to total sales?
-	What are the sales trends over time ?
-	Which geographic regions have the highest and lowest sales?
-	What is the profit contribution of each product category?

- <a href="https://github.com/Fathimathu-swafa/-Adidas_US_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard/blob/main/Adidas_Product_US_Sales.png">Dashboard</a>

## Data Processing
-	Removed missing and duplicate entries
-	Extracted month and year for time trend analysis
-	Loaded into Power BI for modelling and visualization

## Insights
-	Men’s Street Footwear and Women’s Apparel lead in both sales and profit.
-	West Gear is the highest contributor to overall profit among retailers.
-	Sales peak in July and August, showing a seasonal trend.
-	In-store purchases dominate (39.6%), followed by outlet (32.8%) and online (27.5%).
-	Women’s Athletic Footwear is the lowest-performing product in terms of sales and profit.

## Conclusion
-	Targeted promotions should be implemented for low-performing products like Women’s Athletic Footwear.
-	Expanding and investing in the online channel can capitalize on rising e-commerce sales.
-	Stock and discounts should be increased during peak months, particularly July and August.
-	Strengthening partnerships with high-profit retailers such as West Gear and Foot Locker can maximize revenue.
-	Prioritizing logistics and marketing in high-performing regions ensures better sales coverage.
-	Focused marketing and stocking of top-selling products like Men’s Street Footwear and Women’s Apparel drives growth.
-	Implementing an interactive Power BI dashboard with filters and KPIs supports real-time, data-driven decision-making.
